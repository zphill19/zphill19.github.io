Train/Test split¶

train = churn_data[:584]
test = churn_data[584:]

ARIMA model¶

In [11]:
model = ARIMA(train["Revenue"], order=(1,1,0)).fit()

Forecast¶

In [12]:
forecast = model.get_forecast(steps=len(test))
forecast_mean = forecast.predicted_mean
conf_int = forecast.conf_int()

Forecast Visualization¶

In [13]:
